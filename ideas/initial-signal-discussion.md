## Discussion: Microsoft Maia 200 Announcement

Erik shared the Microsoft Maia 200 announcement (Jan 26):
- Next-gen AI inference accelerator
- TSMC 3nm process, 216GB HBM3e
- 30% better tokens-per-dollar than competitors
- Already powering GPT-5.2, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Microsoft Foundry

**Source:** https://news.microsoft.com/maia-200/

### Erik's Analysis

> AI companies (at least the mega providers) are engaging in a silicon arms race.
>
> The danger here is that this may result in methodology lock-in. Today (Jan 26) LLMs are hot. But the foundation algorithms for LLM were well documented until OpenAI had some frame-breaking insights. There may be better approaches that could pre-date the transformer network. Or ensemble approaches may become (probably will) the dominant approach.
>
> In "The Calculus of Thought" Dr. D. Rice goes into great detail about ensemble models - and that was 8+ years ago. Way before LLMs.

**Key point:** Current capabilities are not the limit. They will continue to improve.
