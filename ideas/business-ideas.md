## Evaluation Criteria

When assessing business ideas, consider:
- **Feasibility**: Can we build this with current AI capabilities?
- **Market Need**: Is there a real problem to solve?
- **Scalability**: Can this grow?
- **Team Fit**: Does this align with our skills and interests?
- **Impact**: What positive change does this create?
